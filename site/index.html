    The current job

    One worked problem for every 6th and 7th grade standard

    That’s the proof of concept: 53 standards — 29 in sixth grade, 24 in seventh — each with a real problem, a real figure where it needs one, and an answer the machine computed and checked.

    Not cherry-picked easy ones. The whole grade band, including the awkward corners: negative numbers on a number line, scale drawings, probability models, box plots, surface area of a net. If it survives all 53, it survives anything above.
